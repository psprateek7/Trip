#### Thu 31 Dec — deliberately light ryokan day

- Optional 08:00 market visit or relaxed breakfast, then a short old-town/souvenir walk.
- Transfer to Ryokufuen Kiyoharu as soon as luggage drop/check-in is permitted; confirm the ryokan's exact check-in time and dinner deadline.
- Keep the afternoon and evening for the baths, room, and New Year's Eve meal rather than scheduling sightseeing.

#### Important closure and winter notes

- Takayama Jinya is officially closed 29 Dec–3 Jan, so it is excluded from this plan.
- The Takayama tourist information office lists closure from 30 Dec–2 Jan.
- Hida Folk Village and the morning markets are listed as open year-round, but **Needs verification:** special 2026 year-end hours and any weather-related changes closer to travel.
- Shirakawa-go has heavy snow from late December; use waterproof footwear with strong grip and allow for bus delays.

#### Recommendation and tradeoffs

This sequence avoids duplicating Shirakawa-go's gassho-house experience immediately with Hida Folk Village on arrival day. It gives Takayama's old town the best part of 30 Dec, uses Hida Folk Village as the principal indoor/outdoor cultural attraction while Takayama Jinya is closed, and protects 31 Dec for the ryokan. The tradeoff is that the 30 Dec afternoon is moderately full; skip the Higashiyama walk if the weather is poor or the group wants a slower pace.
