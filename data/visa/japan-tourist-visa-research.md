## Answer in brief

- **Both travellers need a visa.** Indian ordinary-passport holders are not covered by Japan's short-stay visa waiver merely because they live in New Zealand.
- The appropriate category is a **Temporary Visitor visa for tourism/sightseeing**, allowing a stay of up to 90 days and no paid activity.
- **Japan eVISA is not currently available to residents of New Zealand.** The current eVISA residence-country list does not include New Zealand, so plan for a paper application and an original-passport submission.
- Each applicant must hold a valid **long-term New Zealand status** such as resident, work, student or guardian. A person in New Zealand only on a visitor visa is not eligible to lodge through the NZ offices.
- Apply to the office with jurisdiction over your **NZ residential address**, not whichever office is most convenient.

### Sensible supporting material to have ready

These items are not all listed as mandatory for a standard self-funded tourism application, but the consular office may ask for additional evidence: employment/enrolment confirmation, approved leave, payslips, evidence of your NZ address, and an explanation of any large or unusual bank transactions. Do not add unsolicited documents contrary to office instructions; keep them ready and ask if unsure.

## How to apply

1. Confirm that each traveller holds eligible long-term NZ status and identify the correct office from the residential-address jurisdiction.
2. Recheck that office's official visa page, holidays, address, fee and submission procedure in late September 2026.
3. Download fresh copies of the official visa application form and itinerary template; complete and sign one application per traveller.
4. Assemble the documents above as two unstapled sets. Use consistent names, dates, flights, funding and accommodation information across every document.
5. Lodge in person during visa counter hours. Christchurch and Wellington currently do not require an appointment; Auckland currently does. If considering post/courier, obtain the office's instructions first and remember that original passports must be included.
6. Wait for the decision email. Do not book around an assumed approval date: no expedited service exists, and complete documents do not guarantee issuance.
7. Collect the passports as instructed. Current Wellington/Christchurch guidance permits a nominated collector with the official handwritten authorisation and photo ID; Auckland requires every adult to have appeared in person at submission or collection. Passports are not returned by post under the published Wellington guidance.
8. Pay the visa issuance fee at collection if approved. The current special fee for Indian passport holders is **NZD 10 per person** for single, double or multiple entry, valid for applications made 1 July 2026–31 March 2027. Published NZ paper-application guidance says **cash only** at collection. Expected total for two approved applications: **NZD 20**. Recheck before lodging.
9. Immediately verify names, passport numbers, visa type, entries and validity dates. Keep copies separately from the passports.
10. Before departure, complete optional **Visit Japan Web** immigration/customs preregistration. It does not replace the visa.

## Single entry or multiple entry?

This itinerary needs only **one entry**, so a single-entry tourism visa is the simplest fit. Indian nationals can apply for a multiple-entry short-stay visa if they meet the published criteria, but must provide the extra multiple-entry documents plus the single-entry documents; the consulate may still issue a single-entry visa. There is no evident trip need for multiple entry unless your routing changes to leave and re-enter Japan.

## Important cautions

- A visa is not a guarantee of admission; Japanese immigration makes the final entry decision.
- Paid or profit-making activity is prohibited on a Temporary Visitor visa.
- Do not rely on eVISA for this NZ-resident application unless Japan officially adds New Zealand before you apply.
- Never submit screenshots, forms or bookings with conflicting names/dates without a short truthful explanation.
- Do not cancel the 29 Dec overlapping hotel until the trip decision is made, but explain the deliberate overlap in the visa itinerary.
- Fees, processing times, office hours and addresses are live information and should be rechecked in September 2026.
