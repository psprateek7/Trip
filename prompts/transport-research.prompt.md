# Transport Research Prompt

Use this prompt when you want Codex to research routes.

```text
Research transport from [ORIGIN] to [DESTINATION] on [DATE].

Use this project's AGENTS.md instructions. Check official transport sources where possible, cite sources, and compare route options.

Include:
- Timetable or service-frequency notes
- Booking requirements
- Luggage rules
- Estimated journey time
- Estimated cost if available
- Backup option

Update:
- data/transport/transport-notes.md
- data/source-links/source-links.md
```
